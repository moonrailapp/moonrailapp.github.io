PRIVACY POLICY

Your rides stay on your Mac.

EFFECTIVE 21 OCTOBER 2026

Moonrail is a focus timer for the Mac made by Hirdyansh. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and this website. The short version: Moonrail collects no personal data.

WHAT THE APP STORES

Your rides, cargo and the logbook are stored only on your Mac. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

NO ACCOUNTS, ADS OR TRACKING

  • There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app makes no network requests.

PURCHASES

Moonrail Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you.

NOTIFICATIONS

Ride and break notifications are local notifications created on your Mac. They are not sent through any server.

THIS WEBSITE

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may record standard request information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

CHILDREN

Because Moonrail collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
